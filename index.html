You don't know how it happened. One moment you were stuck in traffic, checking your phone — the next, you woke up naked on damp earth, surrounded by people with sun-darkened skin and mud-stiffened hair, speaking a language that existed in no dictionary.

The first months were pure survival. Learning to eat what they ate. Mimicking gestures. Sleeping on the ground, feeling cold that cut through to the bone. Slowly, word by word, gesture by gesture, the language started to make sense.

But something was wrong — or perhaps, strangely right. The seasons passed. Children grew. The young aged. But you… didn't. You could get hurt — the scar on your arm where the boar caught you was proof of that. You could, perhaps, die. But time… time simply didn't pass for you.

The elders noticed first. They whispered among themselves, watched with a mixture of fear and reverence. When the last of the old ones fell, the tribe's eyes turned to the one who never aged. To the one who had seen more seasons than anyone who ever lived.

They placed you beside the central fire. No one voted. No one argued. You simply WERE the eldest. The wisest. The leader.

And now, with the knowledge of 10,000 years of civilization trapped inside your head — but with no tools, no books, no Google — the only thing you can do is speak. Teach. Guide.

How to Play

Type what you want to teach your tribe. The game understands intent, not exact phrases.

Core Loop

1 turn = 1 season (Spring → Summer → Autumn → Winter). 4 seasons = 1 year. Max lifespan ~75 years.

Every turn you type a natural language command in the Council Chamber. The system classifies your intent through 3 layers: keyword matching, embedding similarity, and LLM fallback.

Game Panels

Council Chamber — where you type commands

Stats — population, nutrition, research

Food — caloric sources, stockpile, crops

Health — disease prevalence, practices

People — individual names, ages, hearths

Knowledge — agriculture tree, tools, costs

World — map, biomes, climate

Chronicle — history of your civilization

What Happens Each Turn

You type a command → system classifies it → action is executed

Disease dynamics: natural drift + environment + season + active practices

Food & Nutrition: gathering output → stockpile → nutrition status

Production: firewood and water supply from workforce

Demographics: aging, births, deaths, new pairs

Tools: items in learning advance 1 season toward completion

Agriculture: active experiments gain research points

Religion: influence grows +1.5 per turn passively

Production: Firewood & Water

Two critical resources. Shortages directly impact health, cooking, and survival.

Firewood
production = workers × 200 × biome × season × tools
consumption = hearths × 45 + pop × 6 // +20/hearth winter

Biome: Tropical 1.3, Temperate 1.15, Savanna 0.5, Tundra 0.1, Desert 0.05.

Why: Fuels cooking (raw food → disease), hearth fires (cold), boiling water.

Water
production = workers × 450 × distanceMod × season × tools
consumption = pop × 22 + pop × 6 // +5/person summer

Why: Water supply = hygiene factor → Wash Hands / Clean Wounds effectiveness.

Tips & Strategy

First 10 Turns Priority

Boil Water — biggest disease impact

Wash Hands — hits all 4 diseases + synergy

Dig Latrines — Parasites −15

Make Cordage — unlocks Snares, Fish Traps, Yoke

Quarantine — Respiratory −18 (winter killer)

Digging Sticks — fast +30% roots

Woven Baskets — boosts 6 food sources

Common Mistakes

Ignoring production: Health practices need firewood/water to work.

Overpopulation: Below 60% kcal/capita → Famine.

Winter unprepared: Respiratory +2.5/turn. Quarantine first.

Early culling: Need 10+ influence or edicts are refused.

Key Synergies

Boil Water + Wash Hands: extra Parasites −0.8, Dysentery −0.6/turn.

Cordage → Snares + Fish Traps: Game +40%, Fishing +50%.

Wedges + Cordage: Firewood +40% combined.
